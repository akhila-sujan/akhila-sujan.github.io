EXECUTIVE COORDINATION

Every conversation
starts with preparation.

How can a leadership team organise relevant conference conversations and keep follow-up ownership clear?

  1. Before. Prioritise accounts, coordinate outreach and prepare briefs.
  2. During. Manage availability, time zones and schedule changes.
  3. After. Record actions and route follow-ups to the right owner.
